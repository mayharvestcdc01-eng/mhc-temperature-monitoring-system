3. Counter Checking Entry (All Rooms)

Counter checking is an independent verification of room temperatures by the OIC / PLI. Select personnel and shift, then enter the reading beside each room. The time is automatically captured per room. The system immediately compares your reading with the latest monitoring reading of that room. A difference of more than is flagged as a discrepancy. The counter checker must be a different person from the monitor.

4. Weekly Quality Verification

Quality personnel verify the monitoring records of one whole week (Monday to Sunday) in a single review. Compare the readings with the standards, check the deviations and remarks, then sign. Every pending record in the selected week is marked with your decision. Night shift records follow the advance date of the sheet.
